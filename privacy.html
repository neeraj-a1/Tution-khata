Privacy Policy — Tuition Khata

Last updated: 15 September 2026

Tuition Khata (tuitionkhata.in) is a fee register app built for individual home tutors in India, to help them track which students have paid their monthly fee and send WhatsApp payment reminders. This page explains, in plain language, exactly what information the app collects, why, and what happens to it. If anything here is unclear, you can write to us at the email address at the bottom of this page.

Who this policy covers

This policy covers two kinds of people: tutors, who create an account and use the app, and students and parents, whose details a tutor may enter into their own private register. Students and parents do not create accounts themselves and do not interact with Tuition Khata directly — their information is entered by the tutor who teaches them, for the tutor's own record-keeping.

What information we collect, and why

When a tutor creates an account, we collect:

  • Name
  • UPI ID (used to generate payment links and QR codes so parents can pay the tutor directly)
  • A recovery PIN the tutor chooses themselves (used only to log back into their own account — this is not shared with or sent to anyone else)
  • Phone number (optional) and email address (optional) — used only so a tutor can recover their account if they forget their PIN, and so we can occasionally check in if a new signup goes quiet
  • A photo of their own UPI QR code, if they choose to upload one (optional)
  • Which link or post brought them to the app (for example, a particular Facebook group or ad) — used only to understand which of our own promotional posts are useful, never shared outside Tuition Khata

When a tutor adds a student to their register, they may enter:

  • Student's name, phone number, class, and monthly fee amount
  • Parent's name
  • A photo of the student (optional)

This information is entered and controlled entirely by the tutor, for their own private fee register. It is not visible to anyone other than that tutor.

When a tutor marks a fee as paid, we may store:

  • Which month a payment covers, and the date it was marked paid
  • A photo/screenshot of payment proof, if the tutor chooses to attach one (optional) — for example, a screenshot a parent sent showing a completed UPI payment

What we do not collect

  • We do not ask for or store bank account numbers, card numbers, or passwords to any other service
  • Payments themselves are never handled by Tuition Khata — money goes directly from a parent's UPI app to the tutor's own UPI ID. We only generate the payment link and QR code; we never see or touch the money

Advertising — what we share with Meta (Facebook)

When someone signs up for Tuition Khata after clicking one of our Facebook or Instagram ads, we share a limited signal with Meta Platforms, Inc. — mainly that a signup happened, plus standard technical information Meta collects automatically to connect that signup back to the ad that led to it (such as browser or device information). This is sent through Meta's advertising tools (Meta Pixel and Conversions API) and is used only to measure whether our ads are working and to show our ads to relevant people. We do not send Meta a tutor's name, phone number, UPI ID, or anything about the students in their register.

Where this information is stored

All information is stored in a secured database provided by Supabase, a database hosting service, accessed only by the Tuition Khata app on your device. We do not sell or rent this information to anyone, and the only outside sharing we do is the limited advertising signal to Meta described above. Your device also keeps a small piece of information locally (a login token, so you stay signed in) which never leaves your device.

How long we keep information, and how to delete it

We keep this information for as long as a tutor's Tuition Khata account remains active, so the fee register keeps working correctly. A tutor can remove any individual student from their register at any time inside the app, and that student's information is deleted immediately — without needing to delete the whole account.

To delete your entire Tuition Khata account and all of its data: send an email to upscaleurbusines@gmail.com with the subject line "Delete my Tuition Khata account," and include your name and the UPI ID or WhatsApp number your account is registered with. We will delete your account and all associated data — your name, phone number, email address, UPI ID, uploaded QR code photo, and every student and payment record in your register — within 30 days of receiving your request, and will send a confirmation email once this is done. We do not keep any backup copy of this information beyond that 30-day period.

Children's information

Tuition Khata is built for use by adult tutors, not by children. Student names, classes, and fee amounts may be entered by a tutor as part of their teaching record-keeping, in the same way a tutor would keep this information in a notebook — this information is controlled by the tutor, not collected from the child directly, and is never used for advertising.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this same page with a new "last updated" date.
