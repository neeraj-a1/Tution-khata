WhatsApp + UPI Fee Collection for Tutors: How It Actually Works

No payment gateway, no percentage cut, no app for the parent to install. Here's what's actually happening behind that "tap to pay" link.

What a UPI payment link is

A UPI ID (something like yourname@upi) is all that's needed to receive money instantly, straight into your own bank account. A UPI payment link is just a specially formatted web address that, when tapped on a phone, opens whatever UPI app the parent already has — Google Pay, PhonePe, Paytm, or their bank's own app — with your UPI ID and the exact amount already filled in. They only need to confirm and enter their own UPI PIN.

Why this is different from a "payment gateway"

Payment gateways (the kind used by online stores) usually take a small percentage of every transaction and can hold the money for a day or two before it reaches your account. A UPI link isn't a gateway at all — it's a direct instruction to the parent's own UPI app to pay your UPI ID. The money moves straight from their bank account to yours, the same way it would if they scanned your QR code in person.

How to actually set it up

  1. Get your UPI ID — this is usually already linked to your phone number in Google Pay, PhonePe, or your bank's app; you can find it under "My QR Code" or "Profile" in most UPI apps.
  2. Build (or generate) a reminder message that includes the amount due and a UPI payment link using that ID.
  3. Send it to the parent over WhatsApp — the same app most parents already check throughout the day.
  4. The parent taps the link, confirms the amount in their own UPI app, and pays.

A note on keeping proof

Since the money moves directly and doesn't pass through any third party, it's worth asking parents to share a quick payment screenshot after they pay — a simple habit that gives you a record without needing any separate accounting.
